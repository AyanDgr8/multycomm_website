From scoping to steady state

Six stages, jointly signed off

Every deployment follows the same process-driven path, with a punch list and an acceptance document prepared together at the start so both sides agree what "done" means.

01Scope

Planning & scoping

Expert network planning and scoping that aligns with your business requirements. Our planners analyse your communication needs, manage all dependencies and deliver cost-effective, reliable solutions designed to optimise your network infrastructure — with a customised design tailored to your goals.

02Design

Network design & architecture

Scalable design tailored for growth, with seamless integration into existing systems and third-party applications for high resiliency and maximum uptime. We analyse network dependencies and plan contingencies with alternate traffic routes and effective load balancing.

03Deploy

Installation & commissioning

Seamless, hassle-free installation and commissioning of your cloud telephony solution, backed by a centralised Network Operations Center for proactive monitoring and outage management — a process-driven approach tailored to your requirements.

04Verify

Acceptance testing

Guided by a comprehensive 48-step acceptance test manual, with thorough testing across all processes and dedicated testing of integration touchpoints. We share a detailed punch list for review and sign-off, so you validate every process, minimise risk and achieve a smooth go-live.

05Support

24×7 global support

Comprehensive 360° customer service across two international locations, with round-the-clock Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 support plus 24×7 engineering assistance. Reach us by email, chat, phone or directly through the collaboration platform — and get minute-by-minute ticket updates.

06Maintain

Preventive maintenance

Detailed records of all essential and non-essential updates, timely patching, OS and package upgrades, and major annual feature releases. Each system is tracked individually, so both teams always know the exact version in use and any pending activity.
