CRM integration

Click-to-call where your team already works

The dialer is embedded in the CRM, so there is no tab-switching and nothing to log by hand. Call recordings, agent activity and customer interaction details sync back automatically.

Bitrix

Click-to-call, auto-dialing and real-time screen pop-ups directly within Bitrix CRM. Answer inbound and outbound calls from the mobile app while every recording and interaction is logged back — plus full data syncing of call logs, agent activity and customer interaction details.

Salesforce

Click-to-call and automated dialing inside SFDC, with real-time screen pop-ups delivered without switching screens. Push key call data, agent performance metrics and interaction records into Salesforce, and create, manage and track outbound campaigns from within it.

Zoho

Click-to-call, automated dialing and real-time screen pop-ups without switching windows. The embedded dialer logs all calls, recordings and interaction data straight into Zoho, giving full visibility of agent performance from one centralised dashboard.
