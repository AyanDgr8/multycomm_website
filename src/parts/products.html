MAVE · our AI

MAVE listens to every call, then tells you what happened

MAVE is MultyComm's own voice-intelligence assistant. Point it at a batch of recordings or let it run alongside the queue: it transcribes the conversation in the language it was spoken in, separates the speakers, scores the call against your rulebook and hands the agent the next reply — instead of leaving a supervisor to sample four calls a week and hope.

  • Batch audio transcription — MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG, WebM
  • Speaker-separated, timestamped transcripts
  • Language auto-detect, or pin it for better accuracy
  • Sentiment scored across the whole conversation
  • Call quality scored against your own rulebook
  • Business info extracted — decision, interest, timeline
  • AI Prompter drafts the next reply, with a confidence score
  • Training and config from the same console
  • Every transcript and analysis exportable as CSV
